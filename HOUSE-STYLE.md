# dot.x house style

Updated November 2026. Keep this in sync with every change to the site.

## Identity
- dot.x is the digital studio of Acqua Utilities Media Limited. Brian Lynas is Acqua only.
- Bios, team roles, credits and any professional reference on Acqua or elsewhere use the full name "DotX Representative". "dot.x" only in running copy on the dot.x site and short menu links.
- Lockup: "dot.x · An Acqua company". Footer: "The digital studio of Acqua Utilities Media Limited".
- Titles: Matthew Lynas and Craig Leith, both Co-Founder and Solutions Lead.
- dot.x keeps its own look (black and gold). Acqua keeps navy and blue.

## Copy
- No dashes in copy and no hyphens either: go to market, ecommerce, in house, hand built, pop up. The one exception is co-founder.
- UK spelling. Prices as £1,000, "From £X" with a short line underneath.
- Never name Deeside Golf Club. Benoil and Savuti appear only as "energy sector manufacturer" and "financial services firm".

## Type and colour
- Display: Bebas Neue (var(--display)), uppercase, for headings and card titles.
- Accent: Fraunces italic 200 (var(--serif)) for the emphasised word in headings.
- Body and labels: DM Mono.
- Background #080808, text #f5f5f2, borders var(--border), gold only as hairline trim.

## Structure
- Pages: index (services overview, short about with founder rows, recent work, testimonials, contact), about (studio story, proof numbers, founder cards), brand, web, automation, sport, work.
- Menu: Services dropdown (Brand, Web, Automation, Sport and Golf), Work, About, Start a Project. Same on every page.
- Service pages: page hero, service cards (two columns for 2 or 4 cards, three for 3), one supporting section, "Your project. Next." band.
- All pages are generated from one template so the nav, footer, styles and scripts stay identical.

## Phone
- Mirror every change on phone. Testimonials and recent work swipe sideways; process steps sit two to a row; area cards hide their bullet lists.
- Founders are not named in hero copy, descriptions or bios. Names appear only on their own founder or team cards.
- Matthew is described as a trainee PGA Professional on dot.x only (founder bio, Sport page). Not on Acqua.
- Write "Sport & Golf" (ampersand) in menus, headings, labels and titles. Lowercase "sport and golf" in sentences.
- Services ticker on the home page: seven services only, evenly spaced with gold dot separators, slow loop, pauses on hover, off for reduced motion.
- Case study videos load only when scrolled into view. Keep images under 500KB.
- Founder cards carry round photos (72px, gold hairline), same photos as the Acqua team section.
- Matthew and Craig are co-founders of both dot.x and Vintxge Golf. Never "founder" alone.
- Menus: desktop Services dropdown with numbered items, one line each, plus a single footer link. Phone menu is a full screen sheet: numbered service rows, Studio links, a Part of Acqua card, one Start a project button. Arrows are inline SVG, never the ↗ character (iOS shows it as an emoji).
- Footer: brand line, three columns (Services, Studio, Connect), then a hairline and the copyright row. Same structure on Acqua in navy.
- Tone: professional business face. Sport and golf is a service, not the origin story. Short, plain British sentences.
- dot.x nav links to Acqua; Acqua nav links to DotX Representative.
- Copy: never more than one "and" in a sentence. Split the sentence or use a comma list.
- Every site we build carries the small "Built by DotX Representative" tag with the .x circle in the footer's bottom row, including dot.x and Acqua.
- Opening line of the home page, About and every bio or description starts "DotX Representative is the digital studio of Acqua Utilities Media".
- About lives on its own page on both sites. Home pages keep a short About with small photo rows linking to it.
- Acqua home: hero, three core areas with an "Also available" line, team strip, testimonials, contact. No duplicate service grids.
- Team photos are cropped square with the face centred by detection, on a light ground. Acqua uses team-brian.jpg, team-matthew.jpg, team-craig.jpg; dot.x uses founder-matthew.jpg, founder-craig.jpg. No object-position hacks.
