dot.x Representative is a brand and startup consultancy grounded in direct commercial experience across the sports sector. The practice offers advisory, brand identity, web design and build, photography and brand events to early stage founders, with a particular focus on golf and sports businesses.

The team behind dot.x has first-hand experience of taking a sports brand from concept to a commercially trading business. That experience informs every engagement and is evidenced in the work section below.

Matthew Lynas (Founder and Director) leads strategy, commercial operations and client development. Craig Leith (Co-Founder and Lead Consultant) leads creative, design and digital delivery. dot.x operates under Acqua Utilities Media Limited.

01
Vintxge Golf
Brand Identity E-Commerce Members Club Brand Events Pro Shop Placement International Distribution

Vintxge Golf was co-founded in 2022 as a self-funded golf apparel brand. Brand identity, Shopify e-commerce, product development, photography, supplier partnerships and a content strategy that grew an international community were all built in-house without external funding or agency support.

The brand expanded into physical retail with pro shop placement at Deeside Golf Club, professional golfer sponsorship on the Farmfoods Tartan Pro Tour circuit, pop-up market activations and live brand events. The brand has shipped to over 10 countries with creator partnerships in the USA, New Zealand and beyond.
